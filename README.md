## Current work

### [AtomMail](https://github.com/awhite0030/atommail)

Zero-registration disposable email built entirely on Cloudflare Workers and Email Routing. No accounts, no persistence, no tracking. Designed for privacy by architecture, not by policy.

[Live demo](https://atommail.vercel.app)

### [ERouter](https://github.com/awhite0030/ERouter)

Open-source AI gateway that unifies 40+ providers behind a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Automatic failover, token optimization, and observability for teams that cannot afford provider lock-in.

### [smm-support-bot](https://github.com/awhite0030/smm-support-bot)

Production Telegram support platform with topic isolation, anti-spam, Redis state, and SLA automation. Built to handle high-volume operational queues without manual babysitting.

## Engineering principles

I build systems that remain understandable under load and ownership pressure. Prefer minimal surface area, explicit constraints, and architectures a small team can fully reason about and operate without ceremony.

Privacy is treated as a structural property, not a feature flag.
